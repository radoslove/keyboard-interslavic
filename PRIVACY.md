# Privacy policy — Medžuslovjansky (Interslavic keyboard)

*Politika privatnosti — Medžuslovjansky tipkovnica. (MS version pending language review.)*

**Last updated: 2026-09-03 · Publisher: Radoslove · Contact: via
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/radoslove/keyboard-interslavic/issues)**

This policy covers the Android app **Medžuslovjansky** (package
`com.radoslove.interslavic`) and the keyboard layouts and dictionaries
published in the same repository for Windows, macOS, Linux and Keyman.

## The short version

**The keyboard collects nothing and sends nothing.** It has no network
permission, no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting
service and no third-party SDKs. Everything you type stays on your device.

## What the app does with what you type

- Typed text is passed to the app you are typing in, exactly like any
  keyboard. It is not stored by us, not logged, not transmitted.
- Word prediction and swipe typing use a **built-in dictionary shipped inside
  the app**. Lookups happen on the device only.
- **Optional word collection** ("add words I type to my personal list") is
  **off by default**. If you switch it on, the words are kept in the app's
  private storage on your device. They are never uploaded. The only way they
  leave the device is if *you* export the list as a file and share it
  yourself.

## Permissions

The app requests **no runtime permissions**: no Internet, no contacts, no
storage, no location, no microphone.

Android shows a generic warning when any third-party keyboard is enabled
("this keyboard may be able to collect all the text you type"). Android shows
it for every keyboard. Ours has no way to send anything anywhere.

## Data we receive from stores

If you install from Google Play, Google gives us the same aggregated,
anonymous statistics it gives every developer (install counts, crash
counts by Android version). We do not receive your identity or your typing.
F-Droid and GitHub give us nothing.

## Children

The app has no accounts, no content and no data collection, so nothing here
changes for younger users.

## Changes

If the app ever gains a feature that needs a permission or a network
connection, this policy will be updated **before** that version is released,
and the change will be listed in the release notes.

## Open source

The complete source code, including the dictionary sources, is public under
