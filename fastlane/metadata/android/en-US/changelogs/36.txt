Cyrillic and Glagolitic get their own lj and nj keys, and glides catch short, fast words.

- In Cyrillic the two keys in the top-left corner are љ and њ, exactly where
  the Serbian and Macedonian keyboards have them; in Glagolitic they write ⰾⱐ
  and ⱀⱐ. You can still glide the two letters separately - both ways find the
  word. The q, w and x keys, which no non-Latin alphabet here uses, are gone
  from those two scripts.
- A glide reads the slowest point of your gesture at each end, so short words
  and quick flicks stop coming out as something else.
- Suggestions ignore diacritics while you type - verojetno finds věrojetno -
  and they appear from the first letter. Rare past-tense forms no longer push
  the everyday word out of first place.
- The bottom row follows the iPhone one: the emoji key sits alone to the left
  of a wider space bar, and the comma waits under the period together with
  : ; and the rest.
- The settings screen follows the keyboard's own theme instead of the phone's.
