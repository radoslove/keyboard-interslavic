A glide that finds nothing no longer brings back the word before it.

- When a glide matches no word, nothing is typed - and now the keyboard also
  stops treating the word it typed BEFORE as the one you just swiped. Until
  now backspace then deleted that whole earlier word in one press, the
  suggestion bar still offered its alternatives and an undo chip, and the next
  letter you typed could stick onto it.
- The space the keyboard owes the next word is kept, so the word standing in
  the text is still followed by a space when you carry on typing.
