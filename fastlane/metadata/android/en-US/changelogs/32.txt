Fixes for editing a swiped word.

- Backspace after a glide wipes the whole word only in the first moment after
  it lands. Later presses delete one letter, so an ending can be fixed
  (pisem -> pises) without losing the word. Holding backspace still takes
  whole words.
- No more space appearing out of nowhere: moving the cursor cancels the space
  a committed word owed to the next one.
- Undo: after a whole-word delete, tap the first chip to put it back exactly.
- New launcher icon.
