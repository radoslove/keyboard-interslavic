German, Italian and Polish in Glagolitic; a word translator and learning cards in the app.

- New languages: German (QWERTZ, nouns with a capital, ß held on s) and
  Italian (accented vowels held on their vowel; dell', l' take the next word
  with no space).
- Polish can be typed in Glagolitic, letter by letter or with swipe.
- After a swipe the bar offers the other likely words and endings.
- The app has three tabs: a try box with simple word-by-word translation
  (from Wiktionary, offline), learning cards for your own words, and settings.
- Optional gesture log, off by default, to send with an error report.
