Dutch, French, Romanian and Spanish; Morse code as a script; a learning tab.

- New languages: Dutch (auto's, zo'n), French (AZERTY or QWERTY, l' and
  qu' take the next word), Romanian (ș ț, s-a, într-o) and Spanish
  (ñ key), each with names of people and places.
- Morse code as an extra script for 19 languages.
- The app's learning tab: cards for your own words, the alphabets and
  ready word sets.
- Holding the globe offers the Interslove app next to the keyboard picker.
