Czech, a much larger Polish wordlist, and the settings one hold away.

- Czech is the sixth language; the keyboard now carries 987 006 dictionary
  entries across all six.
- Polish gained its past-personal and conditional forms - zrobilem, chcialbym,
  zebym - which the wordlist simply did not have before, plus first names and
  surnames from the public registers.
- Typing a letter right after a glide joins it to that word only when the two
  can still grow into a word, instead of whenever you typed quickly.
- Hold the globe to reach this keyboard's own settings without leaving what you
  are writing; the keyboard picker stays where it was, first in the strip.
- The settings screen shows which build you are running.
- Suggestions rank frequency properly for Polish and Czech, and the keyboard
  learns your touch offset several times faster in its first twenty glides.
