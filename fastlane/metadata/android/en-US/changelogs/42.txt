Glagolitic with a titlo, three sets of runes, a new learning tab.

- Polish and Interslavic share one Glagolitic layout, with a titlo,
  numerals and undo/redo on the suggestion bar.
- Runes for Norwegian: elder futhark, younger futhark and medieval runes.
- Learning tab: two wheels to pick a language pair or an alphabet pair,
  a cheat sheet of the whole alphabet, full-screen practice.
- Writing tab: tap a language for a word-by-word translation, and
  the script key re-spells the text.
