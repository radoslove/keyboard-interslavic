A native keyboard (input method) for Interslavic — the zonal auxiliary Slavic language, medžuslovjansky.

Standard orthography only (č š ž ě): the four diacritic letters are on a long-press of their base key (hold c → č, s → š, z → ž, e → ě), and the digraphs dž lj nj on d l n. A prediction bar and glide (swipe) typing come from a built-in Interslavic dictionary.

Privacy:
* Nothing leaves your phone. No account, no e-mail, no phone number, no sign-up.
* The keyboard needs no "Full Access" to type.
* Word collection and the popularity ranking are optional, opt-in, on-device only, OFF by default, and never send anything automatically — any export is a file you hand on yourself.

Free and open source. No trackers, no ads, no proprietary libraries — the swipe engine is our own, not Google's gesture blob.

Interslavic itself belongs to no one; this is a free tool to help spread it.
